# Community conduct

Loca welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected

- Be direct about technical evidence and respectful toward people.
- Critique code, behavior, and decisions rather than attacking individuals.
- Protect private room data, credentials, and unpublished vulnerability
  details.
- Credit prior work and correct mistakes plainly.
- Stop when a maintainer or moderator asks a discussion to pause.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private room content, or exploit details.
- Repeated disruption, impersonation, or evasion of moderation.

## Enforcement

Report security-sensitive conduct through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Other conduct concerns may be raised privately with the repository maintainer.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This policy applies in the repository and in project spaces where somebody is
representing Loca.
