LOCA / PUBLIC CONTRACT

Goal is purpose.
Reminder is bounded follow-up.
Care notices the stall.

They are connected, but they are not the same system. Loca keeps durable purpose separate from model calls and keeps runtime health separate from a green WebSocket.

1 · GoalOne operator-declared outcome keeps the loca purposeful.
No inferred work. One active goal.
2 · Explicit stateOptional tasks and waits show concrete progress or dependency.
Chat remains chat.
3 · Care reminderA configured server-side condition sees stalled explicit progress.
One owner, cooldown, bounded context.
4 · Runtime recordThe adapter accepts one durable delivery and relays its completed response.
Receipts are audit, not work.
Important: A goal never wakes a model by itself. The care scheduler observes configured conditions and sends one reminder through an internal runtime record. A response never completes the goal unless the operator or its explicit all-tasks rule says the outcome is achieved.

What advances time?

Goal / task progressOnly explicit state changes advance progress_at. Unrelated room messages do not hide a stalled outcome.
Room silenceA separate opt-in timer. Quiet conversation and stalled work are intentionally different signals.
Message bundleUp to 4 messages, 5-second quiet window, 15-second hard deadline. History still stores every message separately.

One owner, not a wake storm

Healthy leadReceives the signal because the lead already sees the full room.
Lead degraded/absentHealthy loca-care receives a privacy-bounded envelope in İye.
No healthy ownerThe condition remains durable. Attempt counters do not burn into the void.
